Daily Check Queue

Unchecked and due URLs are checked in groups of up to 50 every 30 minutes. Not Indexed URLs stay in the check queue and are eligible again after 30 minutes.

Each run submits only 24-hour due Not Indexed URLs through IndexNow when a key is saved, then checks Google with the URL Inspection API. Bing and Yahoo are still tracked as manual webmaster checks because they do not provide the same public URL inspection endpoint here.
